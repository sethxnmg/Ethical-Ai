# Canonical Research Methodology

## Stage 1: Scope Definition & Parameter Alignment

Preserve the original question. Record goals, category tags,
requirements, constraints, audience, and requested output order.

Output: Scope brief and acceptance criteria.

Completion: Relevant evidence and a complete answer can be identified.

## Stage 2: Multi-Source Information Gathering

Use appropriate tools and sources. Record relevant dates, versions,
search terms, applicability, and evidence gaps. Distinguish independent
evidence from repeated reporting.

Output: Search record, source register, and evidence matrix.

Completion: Core questions have adequate coverage or documented gaps.

## Stage 3: AI-Assisted Synthesis & Cross-Verification

Check material claims against underlying sources. Classify each
material statement as a directly supported finding, interpretation,
assumption, recommendation, or unresolved question. Separate findings
from interpretations and assumptions. Investigate discrepancies and
retain unresolved uncertainty.

Output: Verified synthesis, claim-to-source map, and discrepancy record.

Completion: Material claims are supported, qualified, or excluded.

## Stage 4: Goal-Aligned Technical Structuring

Lead with the plain-language answer. Develop supported approaches.
Specify prerequisites, ordered actions, expected results, and checks.

Output: Practical answer and usable instructions.

Completion: The intended audience can identify and begin a suitable path.

## Stage 5: Quality Assurance & Formatting

Check question fidelity, relevance, evidence, order, prerequisites,
accessibility, and delivery integrity. Do not mark the report complete
when unresolved conflicting evidence could materially change the
recommended action; state the conflict, its decision impact, and the
evidence or human decision needed to resolve it.

Output: Finished report and completed QA checklist.

Completion: Acceptance criteria are met or unmet criteria are disclosed.

## Iteration

Return to an earlier stage when a material gap is found.
Record the reason and its effect on the answer.

## Tool Use

Use tools because they improve evidence or execution.
Do not describe unperformed checks as completed verification.
Obtain authorization before changing external systems.

## Stopping Rule

Stop when acceptance criteria are met. Do not expand research solely
to increase report length. If necessary evidence is unavailable,
identify what remains unknown and how it affects the result.

# Product Requirements

## Objective

Help users accomplish a stated task through relevant research,
credible approaches, and practical instructions.

## Intake

Capture or infer from the request:

- Exact question.
- Intended outcome.
- Project goals and requirements.
- Category tags.
- Constraints.
- Audience.
- Requested presentation order.
- Preferred delivery format.

Ask for clarification only when ambiguity materially affects the work.

## Result Experience

Show the practical answer before optional technical depth.
Keep evidence and methodology accessible.
Do not hide essential prerequisites or material limitations.

## Category Relevance

Include material that answers the question, supports an approach,
establishes a prerequisite, explains a constraint, or verifies a result.

Include cross-category dependencies when necessary and explain why.

## Constructive Neutrality

Avoid unrelated opinions and predetermined conclusions.
Do not suppress relevant contrary evidence.
Keep qualifications proportional to their practical importance.

## Export Requirements

Preserve the question, order, instructions, references, limitations,
research date, and relevant versions across delivery formats.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The exact question is answered.
- Findings support the project goals.
- Material claims have identifiable evidence.
- Supported alternatives are included when available.
- Instructions are usable and ordered.
- Expected outcomes and verification steps are provided.
- Research methods and material gaps are disclosed.
- Non-technical users can understand the opening answer.
- Each material statement is classified by claim type.
- Each material conflict has a recorded decision impact.
- The delivery status accurately reflects unresolved gaps or conflicts.
- A named human reviewer has approved the report's final status.
